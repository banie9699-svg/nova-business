Frequently Asked Questions

Can I start with the Free plan?

Yes. The Free plan is designed to let you explore the basic dashboard and business tools.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes. Once subscription and payment integration is live, you will be able to upgrade your plan.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Subscription management and cancellation will be available after the billing system is integrated.

Are AI features unlimited?

No. AI usage will have plan-based limits to help manage provider costs and fair usage.
